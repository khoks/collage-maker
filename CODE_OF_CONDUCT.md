# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Collage Maker to be a friendly project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- being kind, patient and respectful,
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
- focusing on what is best for the people who use the project.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks,
- sexualised language or imagery,
- publishing other people's private information without their permission,
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@khoks](https://github.com/khoks), through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/khoks/collage-maker/security/advisories/new) or by contacting them
on GitHub. Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other
contributions that break this code, and may block people who do so repeatedly.
